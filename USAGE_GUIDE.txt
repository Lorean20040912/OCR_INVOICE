   .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe ocr_simple.py <image_path>

4. HOW TO PREPARE INVOICES FOR OCR:

   Best results:
   ✓ Clear, sharp images
   ✓ Good lighting
   ✓ Black text on white background
   ✓ Minimal skew/rotation
   ✓ High resolution (1500x2000+ pixels)
   
   Avoid:
   ✗ Blurry images
   ✗ Poor lighting
   ✗ Faded text
   ✗ Colored backgrounds
   ✗ Wrinkled paper

6. EXTRACTION PATTERNS (What the system looks for):

   Invoice Number:
   - Alphanumeric codes: aax5062751, 22x5082751, INV-2026-001
   - 8+ character codes with letters and numbers
   
   Date:
   - Format: DD.MM.YYYY or DD/MM/YYYY
   - Years: 2024, 2025, 2026, 2027
   - FIRST date only (ignores secondary dates)
   
   Amount:
   - Format: 592.82 lei or 592,82 lei
   - Looks for "TOTAL FACTURA" or "TOTAL" prefix
   - Prefers amounts > 100 (to avoid small values)
   
   Contractor:
   - Company names
   - Patterns: "T.D GROUP", "SRL", "SA", etc
   - Found in "Contractor:" or "Contactor:" lines

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

7. TROUBLESHOOTING:

   Problem: Date not extracted
   Solution: Ensure date format is DD.MM.YYYY (with dots)
            Check that year is 2024-2027
            Make sure it's the FIRST date on invoice
   
   Problem: Amount not extracted
   Solution: Ensure format is 592.82 (with point, not comma)
            Make sure line says "TOTAL FACTURA:" or "TOTAL:"
            Amount should be > 100 lei
   
   Problem: Invoice number wrong
   Solution: Must be alphanumeric (letters + numbers)
            Must be at least 8 characters
            Example: aax5062751 (not INV-2026-001 format)
   
   Problem: Text garbled
   Solution: Image quality is too low
            Use high-resolution images (1500x2000+)
            Ensure clear, sharp text
            Good lighting recommended
